# Ground plan — design spec (playtest #26)

Approved by Marcel on 2026-09-22. This is the plan the stadium remap builds to. The maps are
`docs/ground_plan/proposed_L1..L4.svg` (+ `.png`), generated by `tools/ground_plan.py`;
`current.svg` is the build 0.1.1 ground for comparison. **If a number here and the generator
disagree, fix the generator and regenerate; the maps are the source of truth for geometry.**

All coordinates are plot-local studs: origin = pitch centre, +X east (away from the street),
−X the street, −Z north. Pitch, hardstanding ring, clubhouse shell and car park origin are
unchanged from today's code (`Pitch.luau`, `Clubhouse.luau`, `PlotGrounds.luau`).

---

## 1. Principles (all decided)

1. **One fixed ground envelope at every tier.** The perimeter wall, gates, turnstile blocks,
   floodlight masts, huts and fan routes never move. Only the stands, the concourse surface,
   the catering and the car park grow inside/beside it. Routes and turnstile code are written once.
2. **Turnstiles are set into the ground wall, not the street.** Car park, club shop, clubhouse
   reception and the training ground are public; only the ground itself is ticketed.
3. **Stands upgrade independently** (existing `Config.StandPlots`). Only the East stand exists
   at spawn. "L1..L4" maps show every track at that tier for reference, not a forced lockstep.
4. **Buy from the office/HUD Club Map first; see it in the world.** World "for sale" boards and
   project signs also sell. Purchases are locked on matchday, as today. See §6.
5. Utilities (turnstile lanes, toilets, stairs, first aid, stewards, exit gates, groundsman's
   shed, planting) are **never bought** — they appear free with capacity/tier.

## 6. Buying upgrades (retention-driven; see the 2026-09-22 discussion)

- **Club Map** on the office wall and behind a HUD button: this 2D plan as a GUI. Each track
  shows current level, next price, a cash progress bar toward it, greyed prerequisites with
  the reason in words ("needs both West wings at L3"), and a **Go there** button that walks/
  teleports the player to the site. Buying happens here with one tap (mobile is ~75% of play).
- **World boards**: every unbuilt site has a small "for sale" board (the dashed outlines on
  the maps); every built track has a project sign, like today's stand upgrade signs. Same
  prompt, same purchase.
- **Progressive disclosure**: at spawn the map shows only East stand, one more stand and the
  coach's cabin. Tracks appear when their prerequisite exists. Never more than ~5 affordable-
  soon rows; long-term rows (corners, deck, stand over clubhouse) are visible but greyed from
  day one.
- **Dense early ladder**: something buyable nearly every match in season 1 (gravel→tarmac,
  cabin, pitch 1, stand levels). Late tiers are the come-back-tomorrow goals.
- Prices are TEMP and live in `Config`; balance after the loop is playable.
